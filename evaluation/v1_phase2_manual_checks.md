## Shared controller limitations

The Manager's Python controller is shared by V0 and V1 and was intentionally left unchanged. When a tool outcome is rejected or incomplete, the controller can replace the model's final wording with a deterministic status summary; therefore prompt-only naturalness may not be measurable on those turns. Its phrase-based unsupported-save safeguard can also replace an otherwise valid clarification response containing “saved”, “recorded”, “stored”, or “submitted” when no helper outcome exists. The refusal safeguard may replace the model's response for refusal turns. Record these as shared controller effects, not V1 prompt results.

## Recording results

For every turn, record the version and prompt hash, starting preset, customer language, exact synthetic input, model response, helper call and outcome, accepted/staged values before and after, active field, and reviewer notes. Score with `evaluation/rubric.md` only after seeing the actual response. Do not infer quality scores from offline mocks, expected outcomes, or this checklist.
